CampusFind image/performance patch

Changes:
- Allows https://ik.imagekit.io in the Content-Security-Policy img-src directive.
- Uses a small HikariCP connection pool for Neon PostgreSQL instead of creating a fresh DriverManager connection on every query.
- Keeps existing CHAT_DB_URL, CHAT_DB_USER, CHAT_DB_PASSWORD and ImageKit environment variables unchanged.

After applying, run: mvn clean package -DskipTests
Check that the generated WAR contains HikariCP, then deploy through your usual GitHub/Render workflow.
Do not rerun DataMigrationTool or ImageKitMigrationTool.
